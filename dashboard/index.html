Revenue is the sale price of items not cancelled or returned. Gross profit is sale price minus product cost, and margin is profit as a share of revenue. Periods are complete calendar months, compared with the same number of months before. Repeat in cohorts means another non-cancelled order in a later month. All SQL is custom, not a governed definition, over the public synthetic thelook_ecommerce dataset, so growth patterns are generated, not real business results.
